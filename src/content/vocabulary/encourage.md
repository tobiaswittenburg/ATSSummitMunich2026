## Definition

To **encourage** means to give support, confidence, or hope to someone; to help someone feel brave or confident enough to do something.

## Usage

This verb is used when you want to express the act of motivating or supporting someone to do something or to continue doing something.

## Word Forms

- **Noun**: Encouragement
- **Adjective**: Encouraging
- **Adverb**: Encouragingly

## Synonyms

- Motivate
- Inspire
- Support
- Urge

## Antonyms

- Discourage
- Dishearten
- Demotivate
