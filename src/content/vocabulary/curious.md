## Definition

**Curious** means eager to know or learn something; showing interest in discovering new things.

## Usage

This adjective is used to describe someone who wants to learn, discover, or know about something.

## Synonyms

- Inquisitive
- Interested
- Eager to learn
- Questioning

## Antonyms

- Indifferent
- Uninterested
- Apathetic
