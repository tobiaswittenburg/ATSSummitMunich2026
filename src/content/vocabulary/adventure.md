## Definition

An **adventure** is an unusual, exciting, and possibly dangerous activity, trip, or experience.

## Usage

The word "adventure" is commonly used to describe exciting journeys or experiences that involve some element of risk or the unknown.

## Synonyms

- Journey
- Expedition
- Quest
- Voyage
